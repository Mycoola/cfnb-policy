Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

CFNB collects only the OAuth access token required to publish videos to TikTok on your behalf. We do not collect personal information, usage data, or any data from TikTok users.

2. How We Use Information

The access token is used solely to upload AI-generated podcast videos to the authorized TikTok account. It is stored locally on the server and never shared with third parties.

3. Data Retention

Access tokens are stored locally and can be deleted at any time by revoking the TikTok app authorization in your TikTok account settings.

4. Third-Party Services

CFNB uses the TikTok Content Posting API. Please review TikTok's Privacy Policya> for information on how TikTok handles your data.
